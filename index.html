Overview Section

We have the hero that says "We're Taking Tech Back" and a small privacy first callout, but the homepage does not currently explain what we are building or what problem we are solving. The overview section should be the place that does that. This is the first thing a new visitor sees and we want them to immediately understand who we are, what the Cioré phone is, and why it matters.

Goal: Visitors leave the overview section knowing what we build, the problem we solve, and how we are solving it.

Investor and Donations Page

This page is now live. You can view it here: ciore-investor-donations-page.vercel.app

One of the most important things we wanted to get right on this page is the distinction between investment support and donation support. These are two genuinely different paths and the language needed to reflect that clearly. When someone invests, they are entering a financial relationship with an expectation of return. When someone donates, they are offering support because they believe in what we are building with no expectation of a return. The page now communicates both paths in a way that aligns expectations from the start and makes it easy for supporters to understand which option fits them.

The investment support tiers were also adjusted slightly. Because we are still in an early stage of building, the tiers were updated to better reflect where we are right now and what the current landscape calls for. The structure feels more grounded and appropriate for this phase of the company.

Payment links are included as part of the page flow, so supporters can move from reading to taking action without any friction.

Note: The wording around invest versus donate is intentional and should stay consistent across any other pages or materials that reference financial support.

Access Tracking

The internal overview page includes access tracking and logging. This means that visit activity on the page is recorded and can be reviewed. This was an intentional addition. We had a past security concern involving outside contact with members of the team, and having visibility into who accessed the page and when gives us an added layer of awareness going forward.

This is not about creating a surveillance environment for the team. It is about having a reasonable record of access activity that we can reference if we ever need to. The access log can be reviewed through the logging view built into the page.

Note: The tracking captures general access activity and visit data. It is not a guarantee of personal identity confirmation, but it does give us meaningful visibility into page access over time.
