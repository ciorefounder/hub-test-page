Discord HQ
Discord is our primary communication platform and the heartbeat of the Cioré contributor community. This guide will help you set up your profile properly, navigate the server with confidence, and understand how we communicate as a team. Whether you are brand new or just looking to get your settings right, start here.
Display Name Setup

Your display name in the Cioré HQ server should follow this format so the team knows who you are and how to support you:

First Name - Role
Example: Jordan - UX Designer

This helps every contributor and the founder instantly recognize who they are talking to and what kind of work you do. It also helps project managers reach the right people quickly.

How to Update Your Display Name

If you already have a Discord account, use the per-server profile feature so your changes only apply to Cioré HQ and do not affect your other servers.

Step 01
Open the Cioré HQ Server
Navigate to the Cioré HQ server in your Discord sidebar.
Step 02
Open Your Profile Settings
Click on your avatar or username. Look for the option to edit your server profile or per-server nickname.
Step 03
Select Cioré HQ
Make sure you are editing the profile for this server specifically, not your global Discord profile.
Step 04
Update Your Display Name
Set your display name to match the format: First Name - [Role] Contributor or First Name - Role. Save your changes.

Lumé OS
Lumé OS is Cioré's privacy-first operating environment concept built on a hardened open foundation. Research and early architecture planning are underway.
1. Lumé OS: Start Here

Lumé OS is designed to place privacy enforcement directly into the operating environment. Apps do not choose what they access; Lumé governs system permissions, sandboxing, and background activity.

2. Current Direction

The current direction utilizes a hardened AOSP open foundation for early testing and broad hardware compatibility while custom Cioré privacy frameworks are designed on top.

3. Lumé OS Kickoff

Initial kickoff focuses on requirement gathering, boot target evaluation on reference hardware, and security baseline definition. No reference hardware or feature set is finalized.

4. Planning Map
  • Hardened AOSP base evaluation and boot testing.
  • Privacy enforcement layer definition and permissions dashboard architecture.
  • Sandboxing rules and containerized app testing.
  • System UI design, notification management, and settings interfaces.
5. Why AOSP First

Using a hardened open foundation allows rapid proof-of-concept iteration on accessible hardware while establishing core security practices.

6. Workspace Setup

Build environments require high-memory workstations or dedicated cloud build instances capable of compiling full open-source operating system targets.

7. Privacy, Data Control & Permissions

Permissions are explicit, reviewable, and time-bound. Users can audit app behavior in plain language through an integrated privacy dashboard.

8. Accessibility & Waitlist Feedback

User feedback directly informs UI layout, contrast, dyslexia-friendly typography, haptic cues, and plain-language settings.

9. Data Transfer & User Transition Research

Cioré is studying safe, transparent data migration patterns to help users transition smoothly from legacy mobile platforms without exposing unencrypted backups.

10. Research Status & What Is Not Yet Promised

This work is in early research and planning. All concepts remain subject to engineering, security, accessibility, hardware, licensing, carrier, and compatibility review. Not yet a product promise.

11. How Contributors Can Help

Contributors can support documentation, system architecture review, UI mockups, and early kernel/AOSP research streams outlined in Working On Now.

Gaia: AI Transparency & User Control Research
Cioré is studying Gaia as a transparency and user-control research direction focused on plain-language notices, data awareness, and clear permission choices inside modern apps.
Research Background & Origins

Gaia began as research into ethical, on-device AI and related infrastructure concepts. As community and waitlist feedback arrived, users expressed clear concerns: people did not want forced AI, mandatory assistants, or background AI behavior they did not ask for.

Current Research Focus

Cioré is now studying Gaia as a transparency and user-control layer around AI-related activity inside apps. The camera or microphone indicator on standard phones serves as an analogy for a clear, understandable user signal when background processing occurs.

Notices
Plain-Language Signals
Researching how system notices can clearly explain when an app invokes external or background AI models.
Control
User Choice Patterns
Exploring permission controls that allow users to toggle or limit AI activity on a per-app basis.
Data
Data Awareness
Studying what data payloads apps attempt to pass to cloud AI endpoints and how to make that visible.
Accessibility
Multi-Modal Feedback
Ensuring signals use visual, haptic, and audio indicators tailored for all users.
Research Stage Notice
ⓘ
This is active conceptual research. Cioré is exploring these patterns to address community feedback. Gaia is not a finished product feature or promise.
Nyro: Energy & Thermal Research
Nyro represents Cioré's research into supplemental energy harvesting, safer battery chemistries, and passive thermal management. USB-C remains the primary charging method.
Solar Assistance vs. Solar Powered

Cioré devices are solar-assisted, not solar-powered. Integrated photovoltaic surfaces supplement daily standby energy and extend runtime under outdoor light, but wired charging remains primary.

Chemistries Under Study

Research directions include LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) and sodium-ion options to prioritize safety, longevity, and non-conflict sourcing over raw energy density.

Radio and Comms
Exploring offline mesh communication, sub-GHz short-burst radios, and emergency broadcast reception for resilient, local connectivity.
Resilient Communication Research

Studying BLE, Wi-Fi Direct, and ISM-band walkie-talkie style communication modes for local group messaging when cellular networks are unavailable. All research accounts for legal, licensing, range, carrier, and safety limits.

User Control & System Design
Focusing on understandable settings, optional features, user choices, clear permissions, privacy, accessibility, and kernel-level resource governors.
Kernel Governors & CGroups

Research focuses on standard Linux kernel power governors and CGroups resource limits to handle battery, thermal, and app prioritization without background surveillance engines.

Ethical Materials and Sustainability
Searching for healthier alternatives to practices that can harm people, privacy, device longevity, workers, and the environment.
Materials & Sourcing

Evaluating recycled glass, nanocellulose composites, bio-polymers, and modular assemblies with standardized screws to support easy repair and recycling.
